A water town south of the Yangtze at dawn: an old stone arch bridge spans the still river, with a black-awning boat moored beneath it. Thin mist drifts over whitewashed houses with dark-tiled roofs, red lanterns hang under the eaves, and willow branches trail into the water. The surface mirrors the outlines of the bridge and houses, and morning light slants in from the left, forming soft beams in the mist. Photorealistic, 35mm lens, muted blue-gray tones; quiet and poetic.
